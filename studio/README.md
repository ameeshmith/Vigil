# MODES — Award-Winning Digital Agency & Creative Engineering Studio

A modern, high-end editorial digital agency website inspired by the creative direction, typography, and motion language of **modesinc.webflow.io**.

---

## 🎨 Visual Direction & Design Philosophy

1. **Expressive Oversized Typography**
   - Headings typeset in **Syne** (ExtraBold 800 / Bold 700) with tight negative tracking (`-0.05em`) for monumental architectural scale.
   - Body typeset in **Plus Jakarta Sans** for crisp, Swiss-grade editorial legibility.
   - Metadata, time stamps, and indices typeset in **Space Mono** (`01 //`, `VOL. 026`).

2. **Refined Warm Off-White / Cream Palette**
   - Base Background: `#F5F2EB` (warm architectural cream)
   - Secondary Surface: `#ECE8DE` (subtle paper tactile cards)
   - Primary Ink: `#121212` (deep obsidian carbon)
   - Bold Strategic Accent: `#FF3E14` (international vermilion) and `#0038FF` (hyper-cobalt)
   - Zero generic SaaS purple/blue gradients.

3. **Inertial Motion & Tactile Micro-Interactions**
   - **Lenis** momentum-based smooth scrolling.
   - **Framer Motion** line masking, kinetic text reveals, and accordion previews.
   - Interactive custom cursor tracking pointer position with magnetic hover expansion.
   - Asymmetric full-width image grids with smooth zoom and grayscale-to-color transitions.
